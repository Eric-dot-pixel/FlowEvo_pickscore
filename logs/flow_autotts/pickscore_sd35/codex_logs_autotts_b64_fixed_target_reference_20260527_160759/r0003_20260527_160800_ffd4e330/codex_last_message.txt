Implemented a self-contained replacement in [optimal.py](/inspire/hdd/global_user/gongjingjing-25039/sywang/jkcai/FlowEvo/flow_autotts/controllers/optimal.py): a beta-scheduled rival-preserving controller that does `scout -> commit -> ambiguity-triggered confirmation -> single selective backward repair -> late rival/top continuation -> budget fill`, always terminating with `env.answer(rule="best_preview_score")`. The new mechanism is not “more branching”; it preserves one extra late rival at high beta and spends the last compute only when preview evidence is still ambiguous.

The history notes also showed that extra backward-heavy high-beta allocation had recently hurt tradeoff, so this round targets the weakest region, `beta=0.75` first and `1.0` second, with better late evidence allocation rather than more child spam.

Exact beta schedule:
- `beta=0.00`: target `10`, roots `2`, scout `0.2`, commit keep `1`, no backward repair, one confirmation max.
- `beta=0.25`: target `20`, roots `4`, scout `0.2`, commit keep `1`, no backward repair, two confirmations max.
- `beta=0.50`: target `36`, roots `5`, scout `0.3`, commit `0.7`, keep `2`, one repair child from `t=0.6` evaluated at `0.9`, rival tail to `1.0/0.9`.
- `beta=0.75`: target `48`, roots `5`, one SDE scout, commit keep `3`, one repair child, top and rival both can reach `1.0`, stop floor `44`.
- `beta=1.00`: target `64`, roots `6`, one SDE scout, commit keep `4`, one repair child, top and rival both can reach `1.0`, stop floor `62`.
Budget use is monotonic because target NFE, root count, survivor count, repair availability, and late continuation willingness all increase with beta. `beta=1` cannot exceed `64` because every spend is gated by `_can_spend`, which caps total spend at `min(schedule["target_nfe"], env.budget)` and `target_nfe=64`.

What is behaviorally new versus the recent best controller:
- High beta now preserves more committed rivals instead of collapsing early.
- Late previews are score-gap/uncertainty triggered and continue both leader and rival when the contest is unresolved.
- `backward` is reduced to a single selective repair child under ambiguity, which directly avoids repeating the recent losing “more backward/more branching” pattern.
